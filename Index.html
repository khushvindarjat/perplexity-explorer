What is Perplexity? At every step of a sentence, a causal language model predicts a probability distribution over its entire 50,257-word vocabulary. If the model predicted the exact next word with 100% confidence, it experiences zero surprise. If it assigned that word a tiny 0.001% chance, it is intensely surprised.

The Geometric Intuition: Perplexity collapses this sequence of per-token surprises into a single number: the effective branching factor. A perplexity of 14.5 means that on average, the model was as uncertain as choosing uniformly among ~15 equally probable words at every step. Lower is better.

5 First-Principles Guide & Mathematical Foundations

Everything you need to know about evaluation metrics in modern language modeling.

How is the first token evaluated without prior context?

In causal decoder models like GPT-2, we prepend the special token <|endoftext|> (token ID 50256) to the input sequence. The model outputs logits for what token is likely to begin a text document. This provides a genuine conditional prior probability P(token₁ | <|endoftext|>).

Why do we compute perplexity in log-space?

Multiplying dozens of fractional probabilities (e.g. 0.05 × 0.002 × 0.12...) causes floating point arithmetic underflow to zero on computers. Instead, we sum the negative logarithms: Loss = (1/N) * Σ -ln(P), and take the exponential at the very end: PPL = exp(Loss). Mathematically, exp((1/N) * Σ ln(1/P)) is identically equal to (∏ 1/P)^(1/N).

What is DistilGPT-2?

DistilGPT-2 is an 82-million parameter causal language model trained by Hugging Face via knowledge distillation from OpenAI's GPT-2 (124M). It retains 6 transformer layers, 768 hidden dimensions, and 12 attention heads, running 2x faster with 33% fewer parameters while preserving strong language modeling accuracy.

What is a "good" perplexity score?

Perplexity depends strongly on vocabulary size, domain, and tokenization. For a model with a 50k vocabulary, pure random guessing gives a perplexity of 50,257. Natural, highly common English text typically achieves a perplexity between 10 and 35 on DistilGPT-2. Highly technical, misspelled, or syntactically scrambled sentences can yield perplexities in the hundreds or thousands.
